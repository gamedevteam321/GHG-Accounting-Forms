Fossil fuel-derived CO2 exported to satellite precipitated calcium carbonate (PCC) plants

Calculate CO2 exports to PCC plants based on fossil fuel combustion and fraction sent to PCC.

Important Notes:

Biomass CO2 Consideration: Where CO2 exports consist of gases from a fossil fuel fired lime kiln or calciner, the exports of fossil fuel-derived CO2 will be accompanied by exports of climate-neutral biomass-derived CO2.

Approximation: As a general approximation, the amounts of climate-neutral biomass-derived CO2 will be twice the exports of fossil fuel-derived CO2.

Reference: Miner, R. and Upton, B. 2002. Methods for estimating greenhouse gas emissions from lime kilns at kraft pulp mills. Energy, Volume 27 No. 8, pp. 729-738 (Sept. 2002).
